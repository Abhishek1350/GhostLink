## Hero

### Fix broken links on Shopify before they cost you sales

Every broken link is a customer hitting a dead end: 404 pages hurt SEO, damage trust, and quietly reduce conversions. GhostLink gives you a simple way to see every 404 hit and turn dead links into clean redirects — without touching theme code.

- **Watch 404s automatically** — a tiny theme embed logs real 404 traffic.
- **Fix in one click** — create standard Shopify URL redirects from your dashboard.
- **Auto‑Pilot mode** — automatically redirect all new 404s to a safe target (for example, `/collections/all`).

## How it works

### 1. Install GhostLink and enable the theme embed

1. Install **GhostLink – 404 Redirects** from the Shopify App Store.
2. In your Shopify admin, go to **Online Store → Themes → Customize**.
3. Open your current theme and go to **App embeds**.
4. Enable **GhostLink Scout** and click **Save**.

GhostLink now quietly watches your storefront for real visitors hitting 404 pages (broken URLs). There is no custom code to paste and no theme editing required.

---

### 2. GhostLink logs every 404 visit

Whenever a customer hits a 404 page on your store, GhostLink:

- Records the **broken path** (for example `/old-collection`),
- Stores the **full URL** and **referrer** (when available),
- Keeps track of the **number of hits** per broken path,
- Shows the status for each link (for example, Pending or Fixed).

You’ll see all of this in the **GhostLink dashboard** inside your Shopify admin, so you can immediately spot which broken links matter most.

---

### 3. Fix broken links manually or with Auto‑Pilot

You can fix each broken link in the way that suits you:

- **Manual one‑click fix**

  From the GhostLink dashboard, click **Fix** next to any broken path.  
  GhostLink creates a **standard Shopify URL redirect** from the broken path to your chosen target (for example, a collection, a product, or your home page).

- **Auto‑Pilot for hands‑free fixing**

  In the **Settings** panel, you can enable **Auto‑Pilot** and choose a **default target path** (for example, `/collections/all`).

  When Auto‑Pilot is on, GhostLink will:
  - Watch for new broken paths,
  - Automatically create URL redirects to your default target,
  - Mark those links as fixed in the dashboard.

No theme edits. No proxy layer. Just regular Shopify redirects, created for you.

---

### 4. Redirects live in your Shopify admin

GhostLink does not proxy your traffic or invent a custom redirect system.  
Every fix becomes a normal Shopify URL redirect, stored directly in your store:

> **Shopify admin → Online Store → Navigation → URL redirects**

From there, you can:

- Edit redirect targets,
- Merge or delete old redirects,
- Manage your URLs exactly as you’re used to.

GhostLink just helps you get those redirects created faster — based on real 404 traffic.

## FAQ


### Is GhostLink free?

Yes. GhostLink is designed to be a simple, free tool to help keep your Shopify URLs clean and reduce the impact of broken links. If pricing ever changes in the future, it will be clearly communicated on the App Store listing and inside the app.

---

### Do I need to edit my theme code?

No. GhostLink uses a **theme app embed**, which you enable from the **Theme Editor**:

> Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds → GhostLink Scout

There are no manual code changes to make.

---

### Where are redirects stored?

Every redirect created by GhostLink is a normal **Shopify URL redirect**. You’ll find them in your Shopify admin under:

> Online Store → Navigation → URL redirects

You can edit or delete them from there at any time.

---

### Does GhostLink affect SEO?

Broken links and 404 pages can harm SEO and user experience. GhostLink helps you turn those dead ends into proper redirects, which is generally a positive signal for both search engines and customers.

The app itself is extremely lightweight and runs only on 404 pages, so its performance impact is negligible.

---

### Does GhostLink store customer data?

GhostLink works primarily with URLs and page‑level information. It logs:

- Your shop identifier,
- 404 paths and full URLs,
- Referrer URLs (when available),
- Hit counts and timestamps,
- Whether a broken link has been fixed.

We do **not** intentionally collect or store personally identifiable information like customer names, emails, or payment details. Any customer data processed by Shopify remains within Shopify’s systems.

For more details, see the [Privacy](#privacy) section below.
